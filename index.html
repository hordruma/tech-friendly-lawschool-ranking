Methodology

Each school is scored 0–5 per pillar against publicly documented evidence (program pages, course catalogs, center sites), then combined as a weighted sum rescaled to 0–100: Tech & AI Curriculum 25% · Hands-On Legal Tech 25% · Practical Preparation 20% · Research & Leadership 20% · Industry Ecosystem 10%. The bar beside each school is its composite score, segmented by each pillar's weighted contribution. Expand any row for the evidence, per-pillar scores, and sources.

Coverage is curated, not exhaustive — the list focuses on schools with documented legal-tech and AI activity, and absence is not a judgment. Scores reflect public information as of August 2026 and involve editorial judgment in mapping evidence to the 0–5 scale; the underlying evidence and source URLs are published with every entry so readers can disagree. Traditional prestige, bar passage, and employment outcomes are deliberately not ranked here.

Open data & code: github.com/hordruma/tech-friendly-lawschool-ranking (MIT). Not affiliated with any listed institution. Corrections welcome via GitHub issues.
